[CENTER][SIZE=4][COLOR=#F5D78E][B]Why JExVote?[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE][/CENTER]

[LIST]
[*][COLOR=#38BDF8][B]Replace NuVotifier entirely[/B][/COLOR] [COLOR=#94A3B8]- JExVote includes its own Votifier v1 + v2 server. Remove NuVotifier from your plugins folder.[/COLOR]
[*][COLOR=#38BDF8][B]14 reward types out of the box[/B][/COLOR] [COLOR=#94A3B8]- Commands, items, XP, currency, permissions, sounds, particles, titles, teleports, bundles, choice menus, custom hooks, chance, and lucky (jackpot).[/COLOR]
[*][COLOR=#38BDF8][B]Streak milestones + Streak Freezes[/B][/COLOR] [COLOR=#94A3B8]- Day 7, 14, 30+ with escalating rewards. Manual claim mode with interactive GUI or auto-grant. Auto-equipping freeze protection when a day is missed.[/COLOR]
[*][COLOR=#38BDF8][B]Vote Gifting[/B][/COLOR] [COLOR=#94A3B8]- Keep a friend's streak alive with /vote gift. Per-rank daily limit.[/COLOR]
[*][COLOR=#38BDF8][B]Vote Jackpot[/B][/COLOR] [COLOR=#94A3B8]- Weighted prize pool rolled on every vote. Odds and drop history visible in the rewards menu.[/COLOR]
[*][COLOR=#38BDF8][B]Vote Party[/B][/COLOR] [I][COLOR=#94A3B8](Premium)[/COLOR][/I] [COLOR=#94A3B8]- Shared server-wide counter. When the target is reached, every contributor is rewarded.[/COLOR]
[*][COLOR=#38BDF8][B]Vote Shop[/B][/COLOR] [I][COLOR=#94A3B8](Premium)[/COLOR][/I] [COLOR=#94A3B8]- Spend vote points on a curated catalog of items with description preview.[/COLOR]
[*][COLOR=#38BDF8][B]Polished GUIs[/B][/COLOR] [COLOR=#94A3B8]- Vote overview, paginated leaderboard with player heads, streak progress tracker, rewards hub, and vote shop - all from /vote.[/COLOR]
[*][COLOR=#38BDF8][B]Bedrock Edition support[/B][/COLOR] [COLOR=#94A3B8]- Full GUI parity for Bedrock players via Geyser/Floodgate. Native Cumulus forms for every menu.[/COLOR]
[*][COLOR=#38BDF8][B]Offline vote queue[/B][/COLOR] [COLOR=#94A3B8]- Votes received while a player is offline are stored and delivered on next login. Zero lost rewards.[/COLOR]
[*][COLOR=#38BDF8][B]Daily first-vote rewards[/B][/COLOR] [COLOR=#94A3B8]- Config-driven commands and fly-time coupon items on the first vote of the day.[/COLOR]
[*][COLOR=#38BDF8][B]Embedded REST API[/B][/COLOR] [COLOR=#94A3B8]- HMAC-authenticated HTTP endpoints for web backend integration. Per-IP rate limiting and CORS.[/COLOR]
[*][COLOR=#38BDF8][B]Spigot + Paper + Folia[/B][/COLOR] [COLOR=#94A3B8]- Works on all three. Folia-safe scheduling via FoliaLib.[/COLOR]
[/LIST]

[CENTER][SIZE=4][COLOR=#F5D78E][B]Quick Start[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE][/CENTER]

[COLOR=#D4D4D8][B][COLOR=#C084FC]1.[/COLOR][/B] Drop [COLOR=#C084FC]JExVote.jar[/COLOR] into [COLOR=#C084FC]plugins/[/COLOR] and restart.
[B][COLOR=#C084FC]2.[/COLOR][/B] Copy the auto-generated [COLOR=#C084FC]token[/COLOR] from [COLOR=#C084FC]config.yml[/COLOR] into your vote site settings.
[B][COLOR=#C084FC]3.[/COLOR][/B] Open port [COLOR=#C084FC]8192[/COLOR] in your firewall.
[B][COLOR=#C084FC]4.[/COLOR][/B] Add your vote sites to [COLOR=#C084FC]sites.yml[/COLOR].
[B][COLOR=#C084FC]5.[/COLOR][/B] Test with [COLOR=#C084FC]/jexvote fakevote <yourname>[/COLOR].

That's it. Full setup guide in the [COLOR=#38BDF8][B][URL='']Description tab[/URL][/B][/COLOR].[/COLOR]

[CENTER][SIZE=4][COLOR=#F5D78E][B]Commands[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE][/CENTER]

[CODE]
/vote                - Open the vote GUI
/vote sites          - List all vote sites with clickable links
/vote stats [player] - View vote statistics
/vote top [count]    - Leaderboard
/vote rewards        - Rewards & economy menu
/vote shop           - Vote-token shop (Premium)
/vote freeze         - Buy a Streak Freeze
/vote gift <player>  - Gift a streak day (or 'random')
[/CODE]

[CODE]
/jexvote reload                    - Hot-reload all configs
/jexvote fakevote <player> [site]  - Simulate a test vote
/jexvote reset <player>            - Clear a player's vote data
/jexvote resetmonthly              - Reset monthly counts server-wide
/jexvote key                       - Show Votifier key, port, token
/jexvote setstreak <player> <n>    - Set a player's streak
/jexvote debug-services            - Diagnose service-name mismatches
[/CODE]
